Election Verification Rehearsal

Practise how observers would compare a published result package, record disagreements, and stop on failed evidence checks.

Prototype boundary: this rehearsal does not load, verify, transmit, or store real election data. Completing it does not prove that an election is correct, private, accessible, certified, or safe to operate.

Observer instructions

  1. Use at least two observers who obtain the mock evidence package independently.
  2. Read identifiers and digest values aloud, then compare each character rather than relying on colour or position.
  3. Record an accept or stop decision for every check. A disagreement is a stop condition, not a majority vote.
  4. Preserve the package and notes when a check fails. Do not replace evidence silently or restart until the discrepancy is explained.
  5. Have an independent observer explain what the ceremony did and did not prove before closing the rehearsal.

What completion shows

  • The observer sequence and stop conditions can be reviewed.
  • Native controls support keyboard-only checklist operation.
  • Status changes are announced through a live region.
  • The prototype communicates evidence limits before and after completion.

What completion does not prove

  • That any ballot was cast, recorded, counted, or kept secret.
  • That a signing key belongs to an election authority or remained secure.
  • That voter devices, registration, custody, availability, and governance were trustworthy.
  • That the system is end-to-end verifiable, independently certified, or production ready.
  • That every assistive-technology combination works without manual testing.

Trust assumptions for a real ceremony

  • The published verification key is authenticated through an independent channel.
  • Observers receive complete, unmodified evidence packages.
  • Verification software and observer devices are not compromised.
  • Published tallies account for every accepted ballot and rejected-ballot handling is governed.
  • Independent accessibility, security, legal, and operational reviews cover the full election process.

Failure cases and observer response

Package identifier or digest mismatch

Stop, preserve both copies, record their sources, and escalate before using either package.

Missing key or invalid result signature

Stop publication. Record the verifier, key identifier, command, and failure result without exposing secret material.

Tally or audit chain does not reconcile

Do not certify the result. Preserve the evidence and invoke the documented investigation and recount process.

An observer cannot reproduce or access a check

Treat reproducibility or accessibility failure as unresolved. Provide an equivalent independent method before continuing.
